## Notable gotchas (for future reference)

- **Protocol-view schema drift**: the four protocol tables
  (`_DLMS_SMART_METER`, IEC, DNP3, OPC UA) are closed — adding a telemetry
  field doesn't automatically surface in protocol records. Must also add
  the OBIS/LN/point-id entry in the corresponding table.
- **BAD-quality vs absent field**: the DLMS projector historically emitted a
  `None`-valued point when a field was absent (looked like a BAD sensor).
  This would have broken parity if left alone when adding the optional
  `reverse_flow` field. Fix: skip entries whose field isn't present in the
  record dict at all (distinct from `None`).
- **Region discovery**: region twins must be emitted AFTER the full asset
  pass so that synthetic regions (gas, pipeline) are captured — emitting
  them early left `RGN_GASOPS` / `RGN_PIPELINE` as orphan targets.
- **AR1 clamp**: `ar1_step(lo=0)` hard-floored demand. Fix is conditional —
  only drop the floor when `reverse_flow` forced or target itself negative.
